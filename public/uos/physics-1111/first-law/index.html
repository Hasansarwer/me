Used to visualize the final energy level Uf.
ΔU = Q − W,    Uf = Ui + Q − W
Heat sign: \(Q>0\) means energy crosses the boundary into the system as heat; \(Q<0\) means heat leaves.
Work sign: \(W>0\) means the system does work on the surroundings; \(W<0\) means the surroundings do work on the system.
Internal energy increases: The system receives 600 J as heat and sends 250 J out as work, leaving a net gain of 350 J.
